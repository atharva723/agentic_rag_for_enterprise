# NovaRetail Phishing and Social Engineering

- Document ID: NR-IT-021
- Owner: Security team
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: All employees and contractors using company accounts

## Purpose

Use this guide to recognize suspicious messages and callers, report phishing, and protect a NovaRetail account after a click or unexpected sign-in approval.

## Recognize NovaRetail phishing emails, texts, and calls

NovaRetail phishing and social-engineering attempts try to make you disclose information or perform an unsafe action. A familiar display name, company logo, or urgent story does not establish trust.

- Watch for unexpected sign-in links, attachments, or account-lock warnings.
- Treat pressure to approve MFA, install software, or begin remote support as suspicious.
- Check the sender address and destination without opening a link.
- Be cautious when a caller demands secrecy or immediate action.
- IT staff and the Service Desk never ask for your password or an MFA code by phone, chat, email, or in person.

Report a request for a password or MFA code to `security@novaretail.example`, even if the person claims to be a technician.

## Report a suspicious NovaRetail email or text

To report a suspicious NovaRetail email or text, contact `security@novaretail.example` without opening its links or attachments. Preserve the message for the Security team's investigation.

1. Stop interacting with the message.
2. Record the sender, subject or message summary, and time received.
3. For email, attach the suspicious message using the available mail controls if it contains no secrets.
4. For a text, provide a screenshot or its wording without unnecessary personal information.
5. State whether you clicked, downloaded a file, entered credentials, or approved MFA.

Do not send passwords or MFA codes as evidence. Call `+1-555-0142` immediately if you interacted with the message and think company access or data is at risk.

## Respond after clicking a NovaRetail phishing link

Clicking a NovaRetail phishing link requires prompt reporting, even if the page appeared harmless. The response depends on whether you entered information, approved sign-in, or opened downloaded content.

1. Stop using the page and do not reopen the link.
2. Do not run any downloaded file.
3. Report the action and time to `security@novaretail.example`.
4. If you entered a company password, change it immediately at `https://password.novaretail.example` using a trusted company device and MFA.
5. If you approved unexpected MFA or opened suspicious software, call `+1-555-0142` immediately.

For suspected device compromise, disconnect network access and keep the device powered on for Security instructions. Do not reinstall the laptop or delete evidence to hide the mistake.

## Verify a caller claiming to be NovaRetail IT

A caller claiming to be NovaRetail IT must be verified through a contact route you choose, not a number or link supplied by that caller.

1. Ask for the ticket number and the reason for the call without disclosing secrets.
2. End the call.
3. Dial `+1-555-0142`, or extension `4357` on a company phone, yourself.
4. Ask the Service Desk to confirm the ticket and technician.
5. Agree any remote-support session only after verification, with your consent and presence.

Do not rely on a caller's displayed number or knowledge of your work role. A legitimate ticket does not authorize anyone to request a password, an MFA code, or approval of a sign-in you did not start.

## Stop fake NovaRetail IT requests and MFA pressure

Fake NovaRetail IT requests for passwords, MFA codes, or unexpected approval must be treated as suspected social engineering. Repeated prompts do not become safe because a caller says they are testing support.

1. Refuse the request and end the interaction.
2. Reject Authenticator prompts you did not initiate.
3. Record the time, claimed team, and communication channel.
4. Report the attempt to `security@novaretail.example`.
5. Change a possibly compromised password immediately through the password portal.

Call `+1-555-0142` if you shared a password or approved an unexpected request. The Security team assesses the incident; a confirmed security incident is P1 with 24x7 phone support. IT does not need secrets to investigate.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I report a suspicious email?**
A: Send the NovaRetail Security team the sender, subject, time, and safe evidence at `security@novaretail.example`. Do not open suspicious links or include passwords or MFA codes.

**Q: I clicked a phishing link. What now?**
A: Stop using the page and report what happened. Change a disclosed NovaRetail password immediately from a trusted company device; call the Service Desk if you approved MFA or opened suspicious software.

**Q: Someone saying they're IT asked for my password. Is that okay?**
A: No. NovaRetail IT never requests passwords or MFA codes through any channel. End the interaction and report suspected social engineering to the Security team.

**Q: How do I check whether an IT caller is genuine?**
A: End the call and dial the NovaRetail Service Desk at `+1-555-0142` yourself. Ask it to verify the ticket and technician before agreeing to support.
