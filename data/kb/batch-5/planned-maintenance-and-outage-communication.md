# NovaRetail Planned Maintenance and Outage Communication

- Document ID: NR-IT-024
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees using NovaRetail-owned IT systems

## Purpose

Use this guide to find planned maintenance and outage information for NovaRetail-owned systems, understand announcements, and report business impact during an interruption.

## Check whether a NovaRetail-owned system is down

NovaRetail-owned system maintenance and outages are posted at `https://status.novaretail.example` and in the NovaIT Portal at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Use these company channels before assuming a local error affects everyone.

1. Open the status page and find the affected NovaRetail system.
2. Read the affected locations, reported impact, and update timestamp.
3. Check whether the interruption is planned maintenance or a declared outage.
4. Follow the published workaround if it applies to your task.
5. If no matching notice exists, report the exact error and business impact.

Call `+1-555-0142` if a location is down, POS is down, or many users are blocked. Do not wait for a status notice before reporting critical impact.

## Read NovaRetail planned-maintenance announcements

NovaRetail planned maintenance is announced at least 3 business days before the scheduled window through the NovaIT Portal, status page, and email to affected users. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

- Each notice names the NovaRetail-owned system and affected locations or users.
- The start and end times include the applicable local time zone.
- The notice describes unavailable functions and any approved workaround.
- Employees receive instructions for saving work or avoiding interrupted tasks.
- Emergency maintenance can have shorter notice; it is explicitly identified as emergency maintenance.

The Service Desk publishes the notice after coordinating the work. Read the notice for your location rather than assuming every store, DC, or HQ employee has the same interruption.

## Prepare work for NovaRetail maintenance

NovaRetail maintenance preparation should follow the affected system's announcement, especially when transactions or internal file access will be interrupted.

1. Check the announced start, end, and time zone.
2. Save active work before the window begins.
3. Finish system transactions that the notice says must not be interrupted.
4. Inform your manager if scheduled work cannot use the published workaround.
5. Avoid repeated sign-in or transaction attempts while the notice identifies the system as unavailable.

Do not install alternative software, bypass access controls, or move company work to personal storage. If maintenance exceeds the announced end or has a different impact, contact the Service Desk with the notice reference, time, and affected task.

## Understand who declares a NovaRetail outage

A NovaRetail outage is declared by the Service Desk Manager after the Service Desk confirms the affected company system and scope with Store IT or the Security team as applicable. Employee reports help establish the impact.

1. The Service Desk records symptoms, locations, affected users, and workarounds.
2. Store IT investigates store and POS failures; Security confirms security incidents.
3. The Service Desk Manager declares the outage and coordinates communication.
4. The Service Desk posts updates on the portal and status page every 30 minutes during the declared outage.

Each update includes known impact, workarounds, and the next update time. The Service Desk Manager authorizes the restored-service notice after recovery is verified, not simply after one device reconnects.

## Work safely during a NovaRetail outage

NovaRetail outage actions depend on the affected system and approved workaround. Use the outage notice and incident ticket rather than repeatedly retrying transactions with uncertain results.

1. Follow published instructions for the affected task.
2. Report new or wider impact against the existing ticket.
3. Check recorded transaction outcomes before retrying.
4. For NovaPOS offline mode, accept cash and stored-value cards only, for up to 4 hours; card payments are declined.
5. Call for instructions when the offline allowance ends.

A store with POS down is P1: call `+1-555-0142` and press `1`. P1 support is 24x7, with a 15-minute first response and 4-hour target fix. A workaround does not justify ignoring its stated limits.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Where do I check whether NovaERP is down?**
A: Check `https://status.novaretail.example` or the NovaIT Portal for NovaRetail-owned system notices. Report unmatched errors and call immediately for critical impact.

**Q: How will I hear about planned maintenance?**
A: NovaRetail announces planned maintenance at least 3 business days ahead through the portal, status page, and affected-user email. Emergency maintenance is labeled and can have shorter notice.

**Q: Who decides that an outage is happening?**
A: The Service Desk Manager declares a NovaRetail outage after scope is confirmed. Store IT and the Security team contribute to investigation where applicable.

**Q: How often does IT update an outage notice?**
A: NovaRetail posts portal and status-page updates every 30 minutes during a declared outage. The Service Desk Manager authorizes the recovery notice after service is verified.
