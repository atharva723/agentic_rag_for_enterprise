# NovaRetail Lost Devices and Security Incidents

- Document ID: NR-IT-022
- Owner: Security team
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees and contractors reporting company device or data risks

## Purpose

Use this guide to report a missing NovaRetail device within the required time, understand remote protection, and report suspected data leaks or compromised access.

## Report a missing NovaRetail device within 1 hour

A lost or stolen NovaRetail device must be reported within 1 hour of noticing through the 24x7 phone line at `+1-555-0142`. Do not delay the call while searching or waiting for routine opening hours.

1. State whether the device is lost or stolen and whether it is a company laptop or phone.
2. Give the time you noticed and the last known location.
3. Explain whether you were signed in and whether work files were stored locally.
4. Record the incident ticket number.
5. Follow the Service Desk and Security team's instructions.

Company phones can use extension `4357` to reach the Service Desk. Do not include passwords or MFA codes in the report.

## Understand NovaRetail remote lock and wipe

NovaRetail IT remotely locks and wipes lost or stolen company devices to protect company access and data. Full-disk encryption on company laptops does not remove the reporting requirement.

- The Service Desk records the report and coordinates with the Security team.
- IT initiates remote protection; execution depends on the device receiving the command.
- Security assesses account sessions and access that need withdrawal.
- Do not assume a command has executed merely because you reported the loss.
- A found device must be reported against the same incident before you reuse it.

Files saved only on a laptop's local disk are not backed up and can be lost through wiping. Company work should remain in OneDrive or SharePoint.

## Report NovaRetail device theft and arrange replacement

NovaRetail device theft requires the immediate IT report within 1 hour and a report to the police. The police report is not a prerequisite for IT to start protection.

1. Call `+1-555-0142` before waiting for a police reference.
2. Report the theft to the police and obtain the report reference.
3. Add the reference to the existing incident ticket when available.
4. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or confront someone holding it.
5. Ask the Service Desk about replacement and a loaner.

Loaner laptops are available the same day at the HQ IT counter or next business day by courier to stores and DCs. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

## Protect NovaRetail work access after losing a phone

A missing NovaRetail company phone needs the 1-hour report and remote lock and wipe. A missing personal phone with work apps needs prompt work-access protection, not an assumed whole-device wipe.

1. Tell the Service Desk whether the phone is company-owned or personal.
2. State whether it held Microsoft Authenticator, Outlook, or Teams.
3. Ask for the lost authentication registration to be removed.
4. Arrange manager confirmation of identity on a Teams call.
5. Use the Service Desk's Temporary Access Pass, valid for 8 hours, to register a replacement phone.

Personal-phone app protection can remove company app data but does not manage or wipe the entire phone. Never use SMS or voice-call codes as a substitute for approved MFA recovery.

## Report a suspected NovaRetail data leak or breach

A suspected NovaRetail data leak or breach must be reported to `security@novaretail.example`. Call `+1-555-0142` immediately if company data or account access appears actively exposed.

1. Describe the affected system, time, and observed activity.
2. Identify the data classification and intended versus actual recipients, if known.
3. Stop further sharing; do not copy the exposed data into a support ticket.
4. For a suspected compromised device, disconnect network access and keep it powered on.
5. Preserve relevant messages and follow Security instructions.

The Security team determines whether the incident is confirmed. A confirmed security incident is P1, with a 15-minute first response and 4-hour target fix, 24x7. Do not delete evidence or investigate other accounts yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: I lost my work laptop. Who do I tell?**
A: Call the NovaRetail 24x7 phone line at `+1-555-0142` within 1 hour of noticing. Give the last known location and record the incident number.

**Q: Should I search for the laptop before calling IT?**
A: Do not delay the NovaRetail report while searching. The device must be reported within 1 hour of noticing it is missing.

**Q: Will IT wipe the missing device?**
A: NovaRetail IT remotely locks and wipes missing company devices. Commands must reach the device to execute; personal-phone app protection does not provide a whole-phone wipe.

**Q: Who handles a suspected data leak?**
A: Report a NovaRetail data leak to `security@novaretail.example`. Call the Service Desk immediately for active exposure so Security can assess and contain it.
