# NovaRetail Acceptable Use of Company Devices and Accounts

- Document ID: NR-IT-020
- Owner: Security team
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: All employees and contractors using NovaRetail IT

## Purpose

Use this guide to understand permitted personal use, prohibited actions, company monitoring, and the IT response to misuse of NovaRetail devices or accounts.

## Keep personal use of NovaRetail devices limited

NovaRetail company devices and accounts are provided for work. Brief personal web browsing during breaks is allowed if it does not interfere with work, consume excessive network capacity, or involve prohibited content or software.

- Use only the existing approved browser and company device configuration.
- Do not store personal file collections on company storage.
- Do not use company email to operate personal businesses or register personal services.
- Do not assume personal activity on a work laptop is private.
- Store and DC employees use company equipment on site; HQ home working is limited to 2 days a week.

Personal use never authorizes another person to use the device or bypass company access and security controls.

## Prevent unauthorized use of NovaRetail accounts and laptops

NovaRetail accounts and laptops must be used only by authorized employees or contractors. A family member must not use your work laptop, even for a brief personal task.

1. Lock the screen whenever you step away.
2. Keep the device under your control at home and while traveling.
3. Sign in only with your own company account on assigned or approved shared devices.
4. Do not lend your account, badge, password, or MFA approval to anyone.
5. Report suspected unauthorized use to `security@novaretail.example`.

IT staff and the Service Desk never ask for your password or an MFA code through any channel. Treat a request for either as suspected social engineering, not a support requirement.

## Avoid prohibited NovaRetail software and actions

NovaRetail prohibits personal VPNs, streaming apps, and software outside the approved installation process. A personal license or free download is not approval.

- Install company software only through Software Center.
- Request missing software at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`; manager and Security approval are required, with a 2-business-day target.
- Do not bypass encryption, monitoring, filtering, app protection, or device management.
- Do not create local admin access or install a personal operating system.
- Do not use company equipment to harass others, access unauthorized systems, or distribute malicious files.

Report an unexpected installer or suspected unauthorized software. If it might have compromised the device, contact the Security team rather than attempting to hide or remove evidence.

## Understand NovaRetail IT activity monitoring

NovaRetail monitors company devices, accounts, apps, and networks for security, support, and compliance. Records include sign-ins, device health, installed software, and network-use logs.

- The Security team reviews security alerts and suspected misuse.
- The Service Desk uses relevant device and application information for support.
- Company-device monitoring applies during permitted personal use as well as work.
- Personal-phone BYOD uses approved work-app protection, not full device management.
- BYOD app protection manages company app data, not personal photos, personal messages, or the whole phone.

Do not disable monitoring or assume another network makes company activity private. Submit technical questions at `https://itportal.novaretail.example` without personal browsing details.

## Report NovaRetail misuse and follow IT restrictions

NovaRetail suspected IT misuse should be reported to the Security team so it can assess risk and protect company systems. IT consequences can include removing unauthorized software, restricting access, or suspending an account or device connection.

1. Report the affected system, time, and observed behavior to `security@novaretail.example`.
2. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, or unnecessary sensitive content.
3. Call `+1-555-0142` immediately if access appears actively compromised.
4. Follow Security instructions and keep relevant messages or error details.
5. Use the existing support ticket for updates.

A confirmed security incident is P1 with 24x7 phone support. Do not attempt to bypass an imposed restriction or delete records to conceal the activity.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can I use my work laptop for personal browsing?**
A: Brief NovaRetail laptop personal browsing during breaks is allowed if it does not interfere with work or breach policy. Company monitoring still applies.

**Q: Can I install my own VPN or streaming app?**
A: Personal VPNs and streaming apps are prohibited on NovaRetail devices. Approved work software comes through Software Center.

**Q: Does the company monitor my activity?**
A: NovaRetail monitors company devices, accounts, apps, and network activity for security, support, and compliance. Personal-phone app protection is limited to company app data, not full device management.

**Q: Can my family use my company laptop?**
A: No. NovaRetail laptops and accounts are for authorized employees or contractors only, including when the device is at home.
