# NovaRetail Teams Usage and Troubleshooting

- Document ID: NR-IT-018
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees using Teams on company devices or approved personal phones

## Purpose

Use this guide to join NovaRetail Teams meetings, fix audio and video problems, restore notifications, and recover an app that freezes or behaves strangely after an update.

## Use NovaRetail Teams meetings and chat

NovaRetail Teams uses your company Microsoft 365 sign-in and does not require NovaConnect VPN. Personal phones can use Teams only under company app protection.

1. Sign in with your company email, password, and MFA.
2. For a meeting, open the intended invitation or calendar entry and join.
3. Check microphone and camera choices before participating.
4. For chat, verify the recipient before sending a message or file.
5. Use approved OneDrive or SharePoint links for work files with appropriate recipient access.

Do not send passwords, MFA codes, or unapproved sensitive data through chat. Use the "IT Help" channel for quick support questions, not urgent incident reporting or as a substitute for a tracked support ticket.

## Fix NovaRetail Teams microphone or camera problems

NovaRetail Teams microphone and camera problems require checking the selected device, mute state, and device permissions before changing software.

1. Check the meeting's microphone and camera controls.
2. Select the intended headset, microphone, speaker, and camera using Teams device controls.
3. Check accessory connections and physical mute or camera-cover controls.
4. Close another app that is using the camera or microphone.
5. Check through device settings that Teams has the required camera and microphone permission.
6. Leave and rejoin the meeting after correcting the setting.

If the problem persists, report whether the device works in another approved app. Do not download replacement drivers; approved accessory software comes from Software Center.

## Restore missing NovaRetail Teams notifications

Missing NovaRetail Teams notifications can result from app notification settings, device notification permissions, or a status that suppresses alerts. Check each control without assuming that messages were deleted.

1. Confirm that Teams is signed in to your company account.
2. Check Teams notification preferences for the relevant chat or channel.
3. Check whether that conversation is muted.
4. Review your Teams status and any device focus or quiet setting.
5. Confirm that device settings permit Teams notifications.
6. Ask a colleague to send a test message.

On a personal phone, keep the approved work-app protection in place while checking alerts. If notifications still fail, tell the Service Desk whether Teams shows messages when opened and which device is affected.

## Recover slow or frozen NovaRetail Teams

Slow or frozen NovaRetail Teams should be checked by closing the app cleanly, reducing unnecessary workload, and testing a fresh company sign-in.

1. Save work in other apps and leave any active meeting normally if possible.
2. Close Teams completely using the device's application controls.
3. Reopen Teams and check the affected task.
4. If needed, sign out and sign back in with company email and MFA.
5. Save work and restart the company device if the problem continues.

Record whether chat, meetings, or all Teams functions are affected. If credentials are forgotten, use `https://password.novaretail.example` with MFA. Never share a password or MFA code with a technician helping you sign in.

## Reset NovaRetail Teams cache after an app update

NovaRetail Teams odd behavior after an app update can require clearing cached app data after restart and sign-in checks fail. Preserve unsent drafts before resetting app data.

1. Note the behavior and when the app update was applied.
2. Close Teams completely and restart the device normally.
3. Sign out and back in if the problem remains.
4. If still affected, use the device's supported Teams app-data reset function if available. Otherwise, obtain the device-specific cache-clearing procedure from the Service Desk.
5. Reopen Teams, sign in, and test.

Do not delete guessed folders or use cleanup tools. On personal phones, restore the approved work-app protection when signing in again.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: My microphone or camera doesn't work in Teams. What first?**
A: Check NovaRetail Teams mute and device selection, accessory controls, and camera or microphone permissions. Close competing apps and rejoin the meeting after correcting settings.

**Q: Teams is frozen. Should I reinstall it?**
A: Close and reopen NovaRetail Teams, test a fresh company sign-in, and restart the device after saving work. Contact the Service Desk before reinstalling or clearing app data.

**Q: Why aren't I getting Teams alerts?**
A: Check NovaRetail Teams notification preferences, muted chats, status, device focus settings, and notification permissions. Report whether messages appear when you open the app.

**Q: Teams acts strangely after an update. How do I clear the cache?**
A: Preserve drafts and close NovaRetail Teams, then use the supported app-data reset function if available. Otherwise, get the device-specific cache procedure from the Service Desk; do not delete guessed folders.
