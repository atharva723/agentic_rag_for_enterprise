# NovaRetail Account Access and Permissions

- Document ID: NR-IT-003
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees, managers, and contractors requesting company access

## Purpose

Use this guide to request NovaRetail application, shared mailbox, or SharePoint access and resolve permission errors without sharing accounts.

## Request access to a NovaRetail system

To request access to a NovaRetail system, submit a request through `https://itportal.novaretail.example` using your company account.

1. Identify the application, shared mailbox, or SharePoint site.
2. Describe the work you need to perform and whether you need read or edit access.
3. Provide your manager, the resource owner if known, and the required start date.
4. Explain the business reason and any end date for temporary access.
5. Submit the request and retain the ticket number.

The resource owner is accountable for the application, mailbox, or site. Ask the Service Desk to identify an unknown owner. Never borrow a colleague's account.

## Understand NovaRetail access approvals and timing

NovaRetail access requests need approval from your manager and the resource owner before the Service Desk provisions permissions. This applies to applications, shared mailboxes, and SharePoint sites.

- The standard provisioning target is 2 business days after both approvals and all required request details are received.
- A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.
- Time waiting for an approval is not part of the provisioning target.
- Access is limited to the requested work; wider permissions require a separate justification.

Track progress at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. If the completed request exceeds its target, reply `ESCALATE` to the ticket email or call `+1-555-0142` with the ticket number. The Service Desk Manager is notified.

## Obtain shared mailbox or SharePoint permissions

NovaRetail shared mailbox and SharePoint permissions are assigned to your company account, not through a shared password. Specify the resource and access needed.

1. For a shared mailbox, state whether you need to read mail, send mail as the mailbox, or both.
2. For SharePoint, state whether you need to view or edit site content.
3. Submit the access request with the business reason and manager details.
4. Wait for manager and resource-owner approval and Service Desk confirmation.
5. Use your own company sign-in to open the approved resource.

Read access does not include sending mail or editing files. Report missing capabilities against the existing ticket instead of sharing credentials.

## Troubleshoot SharePoint access denied errors

A SharePoint access denied message can mean missing permissions or a wrong sign-in account. VPN connection does not grant SharePoint permissions.

1. Confirm that SharePoint is signed in with your NovaRetail company email, not a personal account.
2. Reopen the intended site or file link and check that it matches the requested resource.
3. If access was recently approved, sign out and sign in again.
4. Check whether the approval covers the site, the file, and the action you need.
5. Send the Service Desk the site URL, error text, time, and access ticket number.

Do not request a public sharing link as a workaround. An unapproved resource still requires manager and resource-owner approval.

## Request a time-limited NovaRetail contractor account

A NovaRetail contractor account requires a sponsoring NovaRetail manager, an end date, and Security team approval. The sponsor identifies the work and access needed.

1. The sponsoring manager submits the account request at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.
2. Include the contractor's work role, start date, end date, and required resources.
3. Obtain Security team approval for the account and resource-owner approval for each requested resource.
4. Allow a target of 3 business days for provisioning after complete details and approvals.
5. Submit a new approval request before the end date if access must continue.

The account is disabled at the approved end date. Contractors use company email, password, and approved MFA, never an employee's account.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I get access to an app?**
A: Submit a NovaRetail access request at `https://itportal.novaretail.example` with the system, business reason, required permission, and manager. Your manager and the resource owner must approve it.

**Q: How long will I wait for access?**
A: NovaRetail standard access provisioning targets 2 business days after complete details and both approvals. Approval waiting time is separate; a business day is Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

**Q: Why does SharePoint say access denied?**
A: Check your NovaRetail sign-in account, the site or file link, and the approved permissions. Send the site URL, error text, and ticket number to the Service Desk if SharePoint access still fails.

**Q: Can a contractor have a company account?**
A: A NovaRetail contractor needs a sponsoring manager, an end date, Security approval, and resource-owner approvals. Provisioning targets 3 business days after complete approvals; the account is disabled at its approved end date.
