# NovaRetail New Hire and Leaver IT Guide

- Document ID: NR-IT-004
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: New employees and managers arranging starters or departures

## Purpose

Use this guide to arrange day-one IT equipment, complete initial access setup, and remove access while preserving company work when someone leaves.

## Request NovaRetail new-hire equipment in advance

NovaRetail new-hire equipment must be requested by the manager at least 5 business days before the employee's start date. A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

1. Submit a new-starter equipment request at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.
2. Include the start date, work role, HQ, store, or DC location, and collection or delivery arrangement.
3. Identify the standard equipment and any role-specific equipment needed.
4. Request application, shared mailbox, and SharePoint access with the required business reasons.

Standard access needs manager and resource-owner approval and targets 2 business days after approvals. For shorter notice, call the Service Desk to agree arrangements; equipment availability is not guaranteed.

## Collect NovaRetail day-one IT equipment

NovaRetail day-one IT equipment depends on the employee's role and work location. The manager confirms the allocation and arranges collection or delivery through the equipment ticket.

- HQ employees receive a company Windows 11 laptop, charger, and headset.
- Design and marketing employees receive a company Mac laptop, charger, and headset.
- Store and DC employees use a shared company workstation unless their manager approves a role-based laptop.
- Company laptops have full-disk encryption enabled and no local admin rights.

Check the supplied equipment with the manager and report missing or damaged items to `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Use only your assigned company account, including on a shared workstation.

## Complete NovaRetail first-day sign-in and file setup

NovaRetail first-day sign-in uses your company email, password, and MFA, with single sign-on for company apps. Follow the initial access instructions provided by the Service Desk.

1. Start the assigned company device and connect to the company network.
2. Set a password of at least 14 characters when instructed.
3. Register Microsoft Authenticator with number matching and test sign-in.
4. Open the approved applications and report missing permissions against the access ticket.
5. Save work files in OneDrive or SharePoint, not only on the laptop's local disk.
6. Install approved software only through Software Center.

Local-disk-only files are not backed up. IT staff never need your password or an MFA code to complete setup.

## Remove a NovaRetail leaver's access at departure

NovaRetail leaver access is removed at the departure date and time recorded by the manager in the leaver ticket. The manager must submit the request as soon as that date and time are known.

1. Create a leaver request at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.
2. Specify the exact departure date and time, work location, account, and company equipment to return.
3. Identify shared mailbox, SharePoint, application, and other access requiring removal.
4. Name the manager-approved person who will take over company work.
5. Arrange equipment return with the Service Desk.

At the recorded departure time, the Service Desk disables sign-in, revokes sessions, and removes company access, including VPN access. For an immediate unscheduled departure, call `+1-555-0142` instead of relying on ticket submission alone.

## Transfer a NovaRetail leaver's mailbox and files

NovaRetail leaver mailboxes and OneDrive files are preserved for 30 calendar days after access removal for work transfer. The leaver cannot sign in.

1. The manager identifies mailbox and OneDrive content needed by the team.
2. Request delegate access through `https://itportal.novaretail.example`, with manager and resource-owner approvals.
3. Transfer required work to an approved shared mailbox or SharePoint site within 30 calendar days.
4. Confirm the transfer in the leaver ticket.

After 30 calendar days, the leaver's mailbox and untransferred OneDrive content are deleted. Existing team files in SharePoint remain in their sites; the leaver's permissions are removed. Files saved only locally are not backed up.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What computer do I get when I start?**
A: NovaRetail HQ starters receive a Windows 11 laptop, charger, and headset; design and marketing starters receive a Mac laptop, charger, and headset. Store and DC starters use shared company workstations unless their manager approves a role-based laptop.

**Q: How early should I request a starter's equipment?**
A: The NovaRetail manager must submit the equipment request at least 5 business days before the start date. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

**Q: When does someone leaving lose access?**
A: NovaRetail removes leaver access at the manager-recorded departure date and time. The Service Desk disables sign-in, revokes sessions, and removes company permissions, including VPN access.

**Q: What happens to a leaver's email and documents?**
A: NovaRetail preserves the mailbox and OneDrive for 30 calendar days after access removal. Managers arrange approved transfer before deletion; team files already in SharePoint stay in their sites.
