# Contacting NovaRetail IT Support and Managing Tickets

- Document ID: NR-IT-005
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: All employees and contractors using NovaRetail IT

## Purpose

Use this guide to contact NovaRetail IT, submit a useful support ticket, track progress, and participate safely in remote assistance.

## Choose a NovaRetail IT support channel

NovaRetail IT support starts with the Service Desk, which handles first-line help and routes store and POS problems to Store IT or incidents to the Security team.

- Submit tickets and requests through the NovaIT Portal at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.
- Call `+1-555-0142`, or extension `4357` from a company phone.
- Email `servicedesk@novaretail.example` for routine help.
- Use the Teams channel "IT Help" for quick questions, not urgent incident reporting.
- Report suspected security concerns to `security@novaretail.example`; call for urgent assistance.

Check `https://status.novaretail.example` for planned maintenance and outages of NovaRetail's own systems. If company sign-in is unavailable, use the phone instead of waiting to regain portal access.

## Contact NovaRetail support during and outside opening hours

NovaRetail Service Desk hours are 07:00-21:00, 7 days a week, local time. P1 incidents receive 24x7 support through `+1-555-0142`; do not use email or Teams as the sole urgent reporting channel.

- P1 includes a store, DC, or HQ being down, POS being down, many users being blocked, or a confirmed security incident.
- For a store with POS down, call the Service Desk and press `1`.
- P1 first response is 15 minutes; the target fix is 4 hours, 24x7.
- Report a lost or stolen company device through the 24x7 phone line within 1 hour of noticing.

Routine questions submitted outside opening hours wait for Service Desk handling during its stated hours.

## Create a useful NovaRetail support ticket

To create a NovaRetail support ticket, open `https://itportal.novaretail.example` and select `New ticket`. Describe the problem in terms of the task that is blocked.

1. Name the affected application, device type, and HQ, store, or DC location.
2. State when the problem began and include the exact error text or error code.
3. Describe whether one person, a team, or the whole location is affected.
4. State whether a workaround exists and explain the business impact.
5. List troubleshooting already attempted and provide a safe screenshot if useful.
6. Submit the ticket and keep its ticket number.

Remove passwords, MFA codes, and sensitive file content from attachments. Call immediately for P1 impact rather than relying on the ticket queue.

## Track and escalate a NovaRetail ticket

NovaRetail ticket progress is available under `My tickets` at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Use the existing ticket for updates so the Service Desk can keep the investigation in one place.

1. Open the ticket to check its status, assigned support team, and requested information.
2. Reply to the ticket email with new error details, business impact, or completed troubleshooting.
3. If the ticket needs escalation, reply `ESCALATE` to the ticket email.
4. Alternatively, call `+1-555-0142` and quote the ticket number.

Escalation notifies the Service Desk Manager. Explain any increased impact, including a lost workaround or additional affected users. If the issue becomes P1, call the 24x7 phone line immediately.

## Verify a technician before NovaRetail remote support

NovaRetail remote-support sessions require your consent and your presence, and the Service Desk logs session details in the ticket. An unexpected caller's claim to be IT is not proof of identity.

1. Ask for the existing ticket number and the reason for the session.
2. End an unexpected call and dial `+1-555-0142` yourself to verify the technician.
3. Agree the session scope and close unrelated sensitive content before granting access.
4. Stay present and enter your own sign-in details privately if needed.
5. End the session if the actions differ from the agreed work.

IT staff and the Service Desk never request a password or MFA code, whether remotely or in person. Report any such request to `security@novaretail.example`.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What's the fastest way to contact IT?**
A: Call the NovaRetail Service Desk at `+1-555-0142` or extension `4357`. Use the phone for P1 incidents; routine requests can go through `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.

**Q: Is the help desk open at night?**
A: NovaRetail routine Service Desk hours are 07:00-21:00 daily, local time. The phone line supports P1 incidents 24x7 and accepts lost-device reports within 1 hour of noticing.

**Q: What do I put in a ticket?**
A: A NovaRetail ticket needs the affected system, location, start time, exact error, number of affected users, business impact, workaround, and steps tried. Never include passwords or MFA codes.

**Q: Will a technician ask for my password?**
A: NovaRetail IT staff and the Service Desk never ask for your password or an MFA code by any channel. Stop the interaction and report it to the Security team.
