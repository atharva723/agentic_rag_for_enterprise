# NovaRetail Working From Home and Travel IT Rules

- Document ID: NR-IT-008
- Owner: Security team
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: HQ remote workers and employees traveling with company equipment

## Purpose

Use this guide to work securely from home or public networks and arrange IT approval before taking a NovaRetail laptop abroad.

## Prepare a NovaRetail home-working connection

NovaRetail home working is available to HQ employees for up to 2 days a week; store and DC employees work on site. Use a company laptop and a home network protected by a password.

1. Connect to your home Wi-Fi or wired network and test internet access.
2. Position the laptop where the wireless signal is reliable.
3. Install required company updates and restart when prompted.
4. Connect NovaConnect VPN for the intranet, NovaERP, or company file shares.
5. Use Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint without requiring the VPN.

NovaConnect is not available on personal devices. Save company work in OneDrive or SharePoint because files stored only on the laptop's local disk are not backed up.

## Continue work when home internet is down

A NovaRetail home internet failure must be distinguished from a VPN problem before changing company software. The Service Desk can check the company laptop but does not administer your home router.

1. Check whether another device on the home network has internet access.
2. Check home router power and cables; restart the router using its normal instructions.
3. If internet remains unavailable, notify your manager that home connectivity is preventing work.
4. Use a managed company phone's hotspot if available.
5. If no company-phone connection is available, coordinate with your manager to work at HQ.

Test internet access before reconnecting NovaConnect VPN. Report a company-laptop-only failure through `https://itportal.novaretail.example` or call `+1-555-0142` if the portal cannot be reached.

## Use hotel or cafe Wi-Fi for NovaRetail work

NovaRetail work on hotel or cafe Wi-Fi is allowed on a company laptop after you verify the network name with the venue. A managed company phone hotspot is an alternative when the network cannot be trusted.

1. Confirm the exact Wi-Fi name before connecting.
2. Use the venue's browser welcome page only for its network-access terms.
3. Never install software, certificates, or device-management profiles demanded by the network.
4. Stop if the page requests a company password or MFA code.
5. Use NovaConnect VPN for internal systems; Microsoft 365 does not require it.

Keep the laptop in your possession and lock the screen when stepping away. Do not install a personal VPN as a substitute for NovaConnect.

## Arrange approval before taking a NovaRetail laptop abroad

NovaRetail company-laptop use abroad requires Security team approval before departure. Notify IT at least 5 business days before international travel; business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

1. Submit a travel IT request at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.
2. Include destinations, departure and return dates, required company systems, and your manager.
3. Wait for the Security team's destination assessment and approval.
4. For a destination classified as restricted by the Security team, use a travel-approved loaner instead of your normal laptop.
5. Arrange the approved device through the Service Desk before departure.

Do not take a company laptop abroad without approval. Loaners are available the same day at the HQ IT counter or next business day by courier to stores and DCs.

## Protect a NovaRetail laptop while traveling

A NovaRetail laptop must remain under your control during travel, even though full-disk encryption is always enabled. Encryption does not remove the need to report a missing device.

- Keep the laptop with you rather than leaving it unattended in a public area.
- Lock the screen when you are not using it.
- Carry only the company work needed for the approved trip.
- Use OneDrive or SharePoint for work files instead of relying on local-disk-only storage.
- Do not give another person your company account or approve their sign-in request.

If the laptop is lost or stolen, call `+1-555-0142` within 1 hour of noticing. The reporting phone line is available 24x7, and IT remotely locks and wipes the company device.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can I take my work laptop overseas?**
A: NovaRetail laptop use abroad requires Security team approval before departure. A destination classified as restricted requires a travel-approved loaner rather than your normal laptop.

**Q: How early do I need to tell IT about travel?**
A: Submit the NovaRetail travel IT request at least 5 business days before international departure. Include destinations, dates, required systems, and your manager.

**Q: Is hotel Wi-Fi okay for work?**
A: Hotel Wi-Fi is allowed on a NovaRetail company laptop after verifying the network name. Do not install network-requested software or certificates; use NovaConnect for internal systems.

**Q: What if my home internet stops working?**
A: Check home connectivity and notify your manager. Use a managed company-phone hotspot if available; otherwise coordinate working at HQ. NovaConnect cannot repair a home internet outage.
