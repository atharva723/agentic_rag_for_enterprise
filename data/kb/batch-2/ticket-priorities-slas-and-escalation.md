# NovaRetail Ticket Priorities, Service Targets, and Escalation

- Document ID: NR-IT-010
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees, managers, and Service Desk agents

## Purpose

Use this guide to understand NovaRetail incident priorities, first-response and target-fix times, and the escalation route for unanswered or higher-impact tickets.

## Understand how NovaRetail ticket priority is assigned

NovaRetail ticket priority is assigned and reassessed by the Service Desk using business impact, affected users, affected location, and available workarounds. The Security team determines whether a reported concern is a confirmed security incident.

- Describe the work that cannot be completed, not only the technical error.
- State whether one person, a team, many users, or a whole location is affected.
- Explain whether a workaround exists and whether it still works.
- Report any increase in impact against the existing ticket.

A first response means a human support acknowledgment and assessment, not the portal's automatic receipt. Target fix means the resolution goal, not a guarantee. Routine support hours are 07:00-21:00, 7 days a week, local time.

## Report NovaRetail P1 critical incidents

A NovaRetail P1 incident means a store, DC, or HQ is down, POS is down, many users are blocked, or there is a confirmed security incident.

- First-response target: 15 minutes.
- Target fix: 4 hours.
- P1 support and target measurement operate 24x7.

1. Call `+1-555-0142` immediately rather than relying on email or Teams.
2. For store POS down, press `1`.
3. State the affected location, systems, start time, and scope.
4. Quote an existing ticket number if the incident was already reported.

Do not wait for routine opening hours to report P1 impact. A suspected security concern goes to the Security team for assessment; do not wait for confirmation before reporting it.

## Recognize NovaRetail P2 team-impact incidents

A NovaRetail P2 incident means a team is blocked or a key system is degraded, without meeting a P1 condition. Report the affected team and the task that is unavailable.

- First-response target: 2 hours.
- Target fix: 1 business day.
- The 2-hour response clock runs during Service Desk hours: 07:00-21:00, 7 days a week, local time.
- A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

Submit the incident at `https://itportal.novaretail.example` or call `+1-555-0142`. Include error details, affected users, and any workaround. If the impact expands to many blocked users or a whole location down, phone the Service Desk immediately for P1 reassessment.

## Distinguish NovaRetail P3 and P4 tickets

NovaRetail P3 and P4 tickets differ by whether an individual is blocked without a workaround or needs how-to help or a request with a workaround.

- P3: one person is blocked and no workaround exists. First response is 1 business day; target fix is 3 business days.
- P4: a how-to question or request, with a workaround available. First response is 2 business days; target fix is 5 business days.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

Use `https://itportal.novaretail.example` for these tickets and explain the workaround or its absence. A request-specific fulfillment target can be shorter than the P4 target fix. Notify the Service Desk if a workaround stops working.

## Escalate an unanswered NovaRetail ticket

NovaRetail ticket escalation notifies the Service Desk Manager when support is overdue, impact has increased, or you need the existing ticket reviewed.

1. Check `My tickets` at `https://itportal.novaretail.example` for a response or a request for information.
2. Reply to the ticket email with `ESCALATE`, explaining the concern and business impact.
3. Alternatively, call `+1-555-0142` and quote the ticket number.
4. State whether the affected users, location, or workaround have changed.

Keep using the existing ticket instead of creating duplicates. If P1 conditions now apply, call immediately and describe the critical impact. An escalation asks for management review; it does not automatically change the ticket's priority or guarantee a fix.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What counts as P1?**
A: NovaRetail P1 covers a location down, POS down, many blocked users, or a confirmed security incident. Phone support is 24x7, with a 15-minute first response and 4-hour target fix.

**Q: How quickly should IT answer and fix my ticket?**
A: NovaRetail first-response/target-fix pairs are P1: 15 minutes/4 hours; P2: 2 hours/1 business day; P3: 1/3 business days; P4: 2/5 business days. P1 runs 24x7; P2 response hours follow Service Desk opening hours.

**Q: Nobody has answered my ticket. How do I escalate?**
A: Reply `ESCALATE` to the NovaRetail ticket email, or call `+1-555-0142` with the ticket number. The Service Desk Manager is notified; call immediately if the impact is P1.

**Q: Can I choose the priority myself?**
A: The NovaRetail Service Desk assigns and reassesses priority from impact, scope, and workaround information. Explain changes on the existing ticket; an escalation alone does not change priority.
