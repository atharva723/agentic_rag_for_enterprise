# NovaRetail Laptop Troubleshooting

- Document ID: NR-IT-011
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees using company Windows 11 laptops

## Purpose

Use this guide for safe first checks when a NovaRetail Windows 11 laptop will not start, runs poorly, or has power, display, or sound problems.

## Check a NovaRetail laptop that won't turn on

A NovaRetail laptop that won't turn on needs a power check first.

1. Connect the approved charger directly to the laptop and a working outlet.
2. Check that the charger connections are seated; bypass the docking station for this test.
3. Disconnect nonessential peripherals.
4. Press the power button normally and note any lights, fan noise, or screen message.
5. If the laptop still does not start, call `+1-555-0142`.

Report no power or a blank screen. Do not open the case, replace internal parts, or use a visibly damaged charger.

## Recover a slow NovaRetail laptop or frozen app

A slow NovaRetail laptop or frozen app should not be repeatedly forced off; unsaved work can be lost.

1. Save work in responsive applications.
2. Close applications and browser tabs you are not using.
3. If one app is frozen, close it through the Windows application-management controls.
4. Reopen the app and check whether the problem returns.
5. Save remaining work, then restart Windows 11 normally.
6. Check for required company updates.

Record the app, task, and time for `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Do not install cleanup tools or change security settings. State whether one app or the whole laptop is affected.

## Reduce heat and fan noise on a NovaRetail laptop

NovaRetail laptop overheating and loud fan noise require checking airflow and workload without opening the device or changing its cooling controls.

1. Place the laptop on a hard, flat surface.
2. Keep visible vents clear of bags, papers, and soft furnishings.
3. Close unnecessary apps and pause nonessential heavy tasks.
4. Save work and shut down normally if the laptop remains unusually hot.
5. Report repeated overheating or unexpected shutdowns to the Service Desk.

Do not insert tools into vents or attempt internal cleaning. If the case or battery appears swollen or damaged, stop using and charging the laptop and contact the Service Desk for replacement arrangements.

## Investigate fast NovaRetail laptop battery drain

NovaRetail laptop battery drain depends on brightness, workload, and connected accessories. Check normal-work behavior before assuming the battery needs replacement.

1. Connect the approved charger and confirm charging is detected.
2. Reduce screen brightness to a usable level.
3. Close unnecessary apps and disconnect unused powered accessories.
4. Check battery-use information through Windows settings for unusually demanding apps.
5. Record approximate runtime and whether the laptop shuts down unexpectedly.

Submit persistent fast drain at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Do not replace the battery yourself. Stop using and charging a laptop with visible battery swelling and contact the Service Desk.

## Respond to a NovaRetail laptop blue screen

A NovaRetail Windows 11 blue screen is a system crash; record its displayed stop code before attempting repeated restarts.

1. Note the stop code and what you were doing when the crash occurred.
2. Allow the laptop's displayed recovery or restart process to finish.
3. After successful sign-in, save accessible work to OneDrive or SharePoint.
4. Check required company updates.
5. Report a repeated blue screen or failure to reach sign-in to the Service Desk.

Do not reinstall the operating system, remove encryption, or change recovery settings. Tell support whether the crash followed an update or peripheral connection. Files saved only on the laptop's local disk are not backed up.

## Restore missing sound on a NovaRetail laptop

Missing sound on a NovaRetail laptop can mean mute, wrong audio-device selection, or an accessory fault.

1. Check laptop and application volume and mute controls.
2. Select the intended speaker or headset through the available audio-device controls.
3. Reconnect the headset or docking station and check its physical mute control.
4. Test audio with another approved application.
5. Save work and restart the affected app if only that app is silent.

Report whether speakers, the headset, or both fail and whether the problem affects every app. Use Software Center for approved device software; do not download drivers from an unsolicited message.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: My laptop won't turn on. What do I try?**
A: Connect the NovaRetail laptop directly to its approved charger and a working outlet, disconnect accessories, and check power indicators. Call `+1-555-0142` if it remains unresponsive.

**Q: Why is my laptop so slow?**
A: Check whether one app or the whole NovaRetail laptop is affected. Close unused apps, save work, restart normally, and check required updates before reporting the problem.

**Q: My battery runs out fast. Can I replace it?**
A: Record NovaRetail laptop runtime and check charging, brightness, and app workload. Battery replacement is handled by IT, not employees.

**Q: What should I report after a blue screen?**
A: Report the NovaRetail laptop stop code, task, time, and whether it can restart. Do not reinstall Windows 11 or disable encryption.
