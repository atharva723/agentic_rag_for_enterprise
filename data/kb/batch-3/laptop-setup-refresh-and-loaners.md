# NovaRetail Laptop Setup, Refresh, and Loaners

- Document ID: NR-IT-012
- Owner: Service Desk
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees and managers arranging company laptops

## Purpose

Use this guide to set up an assigned NovaRetail laptop, understand the 4-year refresh cycle, and arrange replacement or loaner equipment.

## Understand the NovaRetail standard laptop build

The NovaRetail standard laptop runs Windows 11; design and marketing teams use Macs. Company laptops have full-disk encryption always enabled and no local admin rights.

- HQ starters receive a laptop, charger, and headset.
- Design and marketing starters receive a Mac laptop, charger, and headset.
- Store and DC employees use shared company workstations unless their manager approves a role-based laptop.
- Approved applications are installed through Software Center.
- Work files belong in OneDrive or SharePoint, not solely on local storage.

Managers request new-starter equipment at least 5 business days before the start date through `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

## Complete first-time NovaRetail laptop setup

NovaRetail laptop first-time setup uses the company device configuration and your own company account. Do not substitute a personal account or create an unapproved device build.

1. Connect the supplied charger and establish a company network connection.
2. Follow the Service Desk's initial sign-in instructions.
3. Use your company email, password, and MFA for company apps.
4. Register Microsoft Authenticator if your account is not yet enrolled.
5. Check the assigned applications and request missing permissions through the NovaIT Portal.
6. Confirm that work files saved in OneDrive or SharePoint have synchronized.
7. Install required software through Software Center.

Report setup failures with the displayed error. Never provide a password or MFA code to a technician assisting with setup.

## Arrange a NovaRetail laptop refresh after 4 years

NovaRetail company laptops are refreshed every 4 years. The Service Desk coordinates the replacement schedule with the employee and manager rather than requiring an employee to purchase or rebuild a device.

1. Contact the Service Desk if your assigned laptop reaches the 4-year refresh point without arrangements.
2. Confirm the collection or delivery location in the equipment ticket.
3. Save company work in OneDrive or SharePoint and check synchronization before handover.
4. Record any role-specific approved apps needed on the replacement.
5. Return the previous company laptop and supplied accessories as directed.

A refresh does not replace the need to report an earlier fault. Files stored only on the old laptop's local disk are not backed up.

## Request a faulty-laptop replacement or loaner

A faulty or damaged NovaRetail laptop must be reported to the Service Desk so IT can assess repair, replacement, and a loaner for interrupted work.

1. Submit the fault at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`, or call `+1-555-0142` if the laptop cannot be used.
2. Describe the damage, error, business impact, and work location.
3. Stop using a laptop with visible battery swelling or damaged power equipment.
4. Request a loaner if the fault prevents work.
5. Agree the device handover with the Service Desk.

Loaners are available the same day at the HQ IT counter or next business day by courier to stores and DCs. Report a lost or stolen laptop by phone within 1 hour instead of treating it as an ordinary replacement request.

## Preserve the approved NovaRetail laptop configuration

NovaRetail employees must not install their own operating system on a company laptop. IT controls the approved build, encryption, security configuration, and supported application installation.

- Do not replace Windows 11 or macOS with a personal installation.
- Do not disable full-disk encryption or create local admin access.
- Do not reset a company laptop to bypass sign-in or management controls.
- Use Software Center for approved apps.
- Request an unavailable app through `https://itportal.novaretail.example`; manager and Security approval are required, with a 2-business-day target.

If the approved build no longer starts, report the error to the Service Desk. An employee-performed reinstall is not a supported troubleshooting step and can destroy local-only work files.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: When do I get a new laptop?**
A: NovaRetail laptops follow a 4-year refresh cycle. Contact the Service Desk if your laptop reaches that point without replacement arrangements.

**Q: My laptop is damaged. Can I borrow another?**
A: Report the NovaRetail laptop fault and request a loaner through the Service Desk. IT assesses repair or replacement; stop using equipment with unsafe visible damage.

**Q: How long does a loaner take to reach a store?**
A: NovaRetail loaners reach stores and DCs by courier the next business day. HQ loaners are available the same day at the HQ IT counter.

**Q: Can I install my own operating system?**
A: Employees must not install their own operating system on NovaRetail laptops. The approved company build, encryption, and access controls are managed by IT.
