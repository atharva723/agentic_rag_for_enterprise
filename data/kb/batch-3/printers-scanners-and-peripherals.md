# NovaRetail Printers, Scanners, and Peripherals

- Document ID: NR-IT-013
- Owner: Service Desk

- Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
- Audience: Employees using company printers and desk accessories

## Purpose

Use this guide to release badge-based printing, add the company print queue, scan to email, and request or troubleshoot approved desk accessories.

## Print through NovaRetail badge-based follow-me printing

NovaRetail follow-me printing lets you release a submitted print job at any company printer using your own badge. The company print queue is named `NovaRetail Follow-Me`.

1. Open the document in an approved application.
2. Select `NovaRetail Follow-Me` as the printer and submit the job.
3. Go to a company printer and authenticate with your badge.
4. Select your submitted job and release it.
5. Collect every printed page and end the printer session.

Do not lend your badge or leave company documents in the output tray. If your badge is not recognized, report the printer location and error to the Service Desk rather than using a colleague's badge.

## Add a missing NovaRetail company printer

A missing NovaRetail printer queue should be restored using approved company printing software, not a driver downloaded from a search result.

1. Check that your company device is connected to the company network.
2. Open Software Center and locate `NovaRetail Printing`.
3. Install the package if it is missing.
4. Reopen the application and check for `NovaRetail Follow-Me`.
5. If the queue remains unavailable, report the device type and error at `https://itportal.novaretail.example`.

Company printers use badge-based follow-me printing rather than a separate employee-installed queue for each location. Do not connect personal devices to company printing equipment or bypass the company device configuration. The Service Desk can arrange approved installation when Software Center cannot complete it.

## Scan a document to email at a NovaRetail printer

NovaRetail scan-to-email sends a scanned document to the signed-in employee's company email. Check the document's data classification before copying or forwarding the resulting file.

1. Authenticate at the company printer with your badge.
2. Place the document in the feeder or on the scanner glass.
3. Select the printer's scan-to-email function.
4. Confirm that the destination is your company email.
5. Start the scan and check the received document in Outlook.
6. Remove all originals and end the printer session.

Do not scan directly to a personal address or an unapproved recipient. If the scan fails, report the printer location, time, and error without attaching sensitive document contents to the ticket.

## Troubleshoot a NovaRetail monitor, headset, or dock

A NovaRetail monitor, headset, keyboard, or docking station should be checked for power and connections before requesting replacement or changing device software.

1. Check visible cables and connect the accessory securely.
2. For a monitor, check power and the selected input.
3. For a headset, check mute, volume, and the selected audio device.
4. For a dock, confirm its power connection and reconnect the laptop.
5. Test a keyboard or headset directly on the laptop if the dock connection fails.
6. Save work and restart the laptop normally if needed.

Report which accessory fails and whether direct connection works. Do not open the accessory, install unapproved drivers, or use USB storage as a test device; USB storage is blocked by default.

## Request NovaRetail desk accessories

NovaRetail headsets, monitors, keyboards, and docking stations are requested through `https://itportal.novaretail.example`. Standard accessory requests need manager approval and target fulfillment within 2 business days after approval.

1. Identify the accessory and explain the work it supports.
2. State the company laptop type and whether the accessory must work with a dock.
3. Include the HQ, store, or DC location and delivery or collection details.
4. Obtain manager approval and retain the ticket number.
5. For a faulty accessory, describe the problem and checks already completed.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays. Do not acquire unapproved accessories or connect a personal peripheral as a workaround without Service Desk confirmation of its suitability.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I print using my badge?**
A: Submit the document to `NovaRetail Follow-Me`, authenticate with your badge at any company printer, and release your job. Collect the pages before ending the session.

**Q: I can't see a printer. What should I install?**
A: Install `NovaRetail Printing` from Software Center on the company network. Reopen the application and check for `NovaRetail Follow-Me`; report continuing failures to the Service Desk.

**Q: How do I scan something to my email?**
A: At a NovaRetail printer, sign in with your badge, select scan-to-email, confirm your company address, and start the scan. Collect originals and check the file in Outlook.

**Q: Where do I get a monitor or headset?**
A: Request NovaRetail accessories through the NovaIT Portal with manager approval. Standard fulfillment targets 2 business days after approval.
